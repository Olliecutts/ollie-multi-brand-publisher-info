Ollie Multi-Brand Publisher

A local publishing tool for Ollie’s own character brands, beginning with Puddle & Boone.

The tool checks an authorised YouTube channel, reads its video inventory and publication status, and can upload approved videos with their approved titles, descriptions and schedules through the official YouTube API.

Each brand has its own media approvals, account connection and publishing records. Routine scheduling runs locally without an AI model call. Publishing routes are enabled only after they have been tested and authorised.

This is a private operator tool. It does not offer public sign-ups or access to other people’s channels.
