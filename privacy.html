Privacy and Google account data

Effective date: 15 October 2026.

Ollie Multi-Brand Publisher is operated by Ollie for authorised, operator-controlled social accounts.

What the tool accesses

Google authorisation requests YouTube upload and read-only access. The tool uses the selected channel’s identifier, upload inventory, video metadata, file details available to the channel owner, processing status and scheduled or published status. These reads identify the correct account, check for existing copies and verify submissions.

How data is used and stored

Authorisation credentials are stored in restricted local files. Channel identities, approved metadata, media hashes, schedules and submission receipts are kept in local configuration and publishing records. These records support account separation, verification and duplicate prevention.

Approved videos and their metadata are sent to YouTube when the operator has authorised a submission. Google also receives requests to authorise access, refresh credentials and read channel data. The tool does not sell Google user data, send it to advertisers, or use it to train AI models. Routine publication does not send Google user data to an AI model.

Retention and removal

Local credentials and publishing records remain until the operator removes them; there is no automatic retention deadline. To disconnect Google access, revoke the app through your Google Account’s third-party connections and remove its local credential file. Stop the local worker before changing credentials or records. Removing local records does not delete videos already submitted to YouTube.

This website

These static pages have no forms, tracking scripts or publisher account login. The hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may process visitor connection information under GitHub’s privacy statement. No credentials, publishing records or videos are hosted on this site.

Contact

Contact the operator through Ollie’s GitHub profile. Never put credentials or private publishing records in public issues.
